# Voiceover Narration And Sync

Lock a human-readable script, speaking rate, pauses and breath points. Prefer user-recorded voice for founder identity; run noise cleanup, compression, EQ and audio alignment. Local TTS only after checking runtime availability, voice weights license and commercial permissions; don't pretend it is the user's private cloned voice. Transcribe actual final recording and align word timings for screen actions and short subtitles. Illustrations are not genuine lip-synced talking avatars; no voiceover means disclose instrumental-only edit.
