# Social Video Growth & Versioning
Select video objective and output per placement. Common initial masters: 9:16 1080x1920, 1:1 1080x1080, 4:5 1080x1350, 16:9 1920x1080, subject to actual platform requirements and render capacity.
- Produce 2-4 real creative hooks, not only color-swapped templates. Structure openings around a specific viewer problem and one testable CTA.
- For silent autoplay, ensure visual copy explains the story. Preserve platform cutouts and title safe zones, add caption variants where appropriate.
- Capture aspect-specific crops via separate layout logic rather than stretching a single video. Keep metrics/status from actual published posts separate from hypothesized expected performance.
- Output `platform | dimensions | length | title | opening hook | CTA | file` and include linked artifacts only if they exist.
