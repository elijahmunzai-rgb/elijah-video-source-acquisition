# Brand and Asset Governance
Separate client identities, credentials, fonts, licensed footage, logos and visual claims. For IO Agency use only approved assets; for Apex Cloud Development use Apex's brand identity instead. Do not transfer work or proof between them without explicit basis.
- Make a provenance table: asset | source | permission/license | attribution | alterations | destination. An asset's being on the internet does not grant video reuse rights.
- Asset packs and open-source libraries have independent licenses: CC0 directory lists do not imply linked projects or their imagery are CC0.
- Keep sensitive records and people out of demo media unless permission and need are established. No fake claims, clients, metrics, or brand affiliations.
- No user font file redistribution. Bundled source must not include ripped fonts, third-party music tracks, brand logos, or private content.
