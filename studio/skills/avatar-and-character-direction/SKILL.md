# Avatar And Character Direction

Use actual approved IO-only logo and original three-pose mascot when relevant. Graphic cutout still is not photorealistic actor. Do not invent digital twin animation. Use still mascot as one editorial element only, not as every scene's substitute for footage. Inspect cutout alpha edges, lighting and optical size. A lip-synced performance needs genuine captured video or authorized model producing real aligned frames. Preserve identity and no misleading voice attribution.
