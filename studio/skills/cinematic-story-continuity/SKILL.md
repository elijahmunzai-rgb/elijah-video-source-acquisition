# Cinematic Story Continuity
Maintain a continuity bible for characters, props, lighting cues, camera direction, screen axis, spatial coordinates, visual motif, and CTA reveal. For one-take motion use a single persistent anchor or path, not disconnected cards disguised as a story.
- Keyframes list the emotional and spatial state at each scene handoff. Avoid temporal jumps unless deliberately editorial.
- For generated/stock footage verify rights, aspect, matching motion and lighting. Code-only abstract scenes can provide cinematic pacing without photorealistic AI generation.
- Track all on-screen claims and customer data through scene changes; proof must be real or clearly illustrative.
- Confirm object entry/exit bounds with full-frame capture. Keep key movement away from mobile platform UI overlays.
