# Motion Graphics Art Director
Choose 1 or 2 motion principles per scene: spatial momentum, follow-through, reveal masks, overshoot, elastic easing, kinetic typography, trim-path line drawing, parallax or orbit.
- Use 3D-feeling depth with shadows and occlusion only if contrast and performance remain solid. Avoid default cyber-neon, overwhelming glow, generic template grids and constant bouncing.
- Arrange hierarchy across 3 scales: dominant headline, secondary evidence, quiet support; maintain generous negative space and mobile safe zones.
- Establish a consistent motion system: entrance curve, exit curve, relative timing, spatial direction, and color-role tokens. Text must not flicker, clip, spill outside frame, or overlap CTA.
- Rhythm: strong stillness moments between bursts, transition only when motivated by story. Test individual frame stills rather than evaluating movement on thumbnails alone.
Deliver layer specification per beat with exact movement vectors, easing, and supported implementation route.
