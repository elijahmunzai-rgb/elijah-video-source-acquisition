# Free Footage Acquisition And Clearance

Prefer authentic authorized SDR/BDR/VA footage or permissioned screen recording, then genuinely free commercial-use stock. Build a source ledger asset_id/source URL/license URL/creator/as-of/original resolution/crop/trademarks/model rights. Check legal terms before use. Pexels official license is one possible source (https://www.pexels.com/legal-pages/license/): permits free use and editing, forbids misleading endorsement. Stock must match shots/action and grade; random office stock creates a low-value montage. Do not claim inaccessible/download-only assets were actually obtained. If there is insufficient actual footage, make a 3-6s animatic and solve assets before full export.
