## 5. Quality-first production operating procedure

**G0** Verified brief, audience/brand/claims, required aspect and budget; public vs private use.
**G1** Original narrative, real opening hook, shot-by-shot script, story/character bible, camera path, visual and audio references.
**G2** Inventory source media, assets, locations, performers and narration. Secure rights and releases. Verify actual footage quality before building the edit.
**G3** Shoot/render **one strong 3–6-second source-media-led visual hook with real/reviewed soundtrack/VO**. Manual critique: story, camera, pixels/assets, typography/compositing, audio, originality/brand. EACH >=4/5 before progressing. An attractive waveform or abstract text slide **cannot** stand in for real footage if brief demands real actors.
**G4** Assemble varied actual scenes, consistent character/location/lighting, proper transitions and captions, final VO/SFX/music, then 30-second commercial.
**G5** Technical ffprobe and creative review: true export 1080p minimum when source warrants, no looping clones, color, peak audio/loudness, lip-sync if relevant, safe areas for vertical/mobile.
**G6** Only after user approves 30-second true content, scale to authored 2-minute and then 10-minute projects. No loops or staged placeholders passed off as complete.
**G7** Delivery/approval gate, explicit release rights, archive project source and evidence. No automatic publishing of client video.
