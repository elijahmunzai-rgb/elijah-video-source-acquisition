01 · Creative brief

Custom planning up to 60 minutes. Single public CPU render requests currently capped at 10 minutes and may require segmenting; 5/10-minute films need a substantial cleared shot library.

02 · Shot-by-shot editing timeline

Each scene needs a distinct, rights-cleared video segment, its own copy and duration. You can plan all lengths; long-form rendering remains blocked until sufficient unique footage is available.

03 · Narration and score

The built-in eSpeak voice is intelligible but synthetic and not an approved professional advertising narrator. Recorded voice/music upload requires future authenticated private storage; this public prototype cannot handle client audio safely.
Scene narration is editable inside each shot. Mix voice and score on the rendering worker, not on Hostinger's website server.
